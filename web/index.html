Find your rhythm.

  1. Set your table rules. Match the rules to your practice game.
  2. Start your webcam. Choose Browser webcam and allow camera access. No microphone access is requested.
  3. Enter every exposed card. Select its source, then click a rank. Camera recognition is not active yet.
  4. Check your decision. The recommendation uses basic strategy after the dealer has checked for blackjack.
  5. Move to the next hand. Your running count continues until you start a new shoe.

To practice splits, use Fix on a card to move it to another hand ID, mark each split hand “From a split,” then add its drawn cards. Cards stay counted once.

Camera trouble? Use localhost, allow camera access in your browser and operating system, and close other apps using the webcam. OpenCV mode uses the camera permission of the terminal running Python.

Shortcuts: A, 2–9, 0 or T, J, Q, K enter ranks. U undoes. N starts the next hand. Shortcuts pause while editing a form.
